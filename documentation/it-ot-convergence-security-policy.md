# IT/OT Convergence Security Policy

## Purpose

Establish governance requirements for protecting connected corporate IT and industrial OT environments while maintaining safety, availability, and production continuity.

## Scope

This policy applies to corporate networks, industrial control systems, HMIs, PLCs, engineering workstations, remote access mechanisms, plant personnel, vendors, and supporting service providers.

## Policy Requirements

1. Corporate IT and industrial OT networks must be separated using documented zones, conduits, DMZs, and approved security controls.
2. Direct and unnecessary inbound communication from IT to OT environments is prohibited.
3. Industrial assets must not use unchanged factory-default credentials.
4. Shared vendor and engineering accounts must be eliminated or formally controlled through documented exceptions.
5. Remote access must use approved jump boxes, strong authentication, session monitoring, and time-bound authorization.
6. OT incident-response procedures must account for physical safety, production availability, and manual operating requirements.
7. Tabletop exercises and access reviews must be documented and tracked to completion.
8. Exceptions require documented risk acceptance, compensating controls, an owner, and an expiration date.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in access removal, retraining, vendor escalation, corrective action, or formal risk acceptance review.
