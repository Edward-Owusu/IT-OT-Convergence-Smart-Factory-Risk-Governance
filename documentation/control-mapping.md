## Control Objectives

### CNT-OT-101 — Unidirectional Perimeter Architecture

Restrict direct communication from corporate IT networks to factory automation assets. Permit only approved and monitored diagnostic flows through controlled architecture.

### CNT-OT-102 — Industrial Asset Access Governance

Eliminate shared or generic vendor passwords on PLCs and industrial assets. Require unique engineer credentials with controlled provisioning, rotation, and review.

### CNT-OT-103 — Cyber-Physical Emergency Isolation Playbook

Define safety-aware isolation triggers and manual override responsibilities when corporate Active Directory, network services, or connected systems are affected by ransomware.
