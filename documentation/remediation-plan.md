# Remediation and Governance Plan

## Priority 1 — Strengthen IT/OT Network Separation

- Validate all IT-to-OT communication paths.
- Review firewall rules and jump-box access.
- Document approved zones, conduits, and diagnostic flows.
- Establish recurring firewall and architecture reviews.

## Priority 2 — Improve Industrial Identity Governance

- Inventory HMI, PLC, and engineering workstation accounts.
- Remove or disable factory-default and shared credentials.
- Implement unique user profiles and controlled privileged access.
- Review credentials and access rights quarterly.

## Priority 3 — Operationalize OT Incident Response

- Create safety-aware containment and isolation criteria.
- Define responsibilities for IT, OT engineering, plant operations, and leadership.
- Conduct annual tabletop exercises.
- Track action items to closure and update playbooks after exercises.

## Suggested Metrics

- Percentage of industrial assets with unique named accounts
- Percentage of reviewed firewall rules with documented business justification
- Number of unauthorized IT-to-OT connection attempts
- Percentage of tabletop exercise actions closed on time
- Percentage of critical OT assets covered by approved response procedures
