# IT/OT Convergence & Smart Factory Risk Governance

## Project Overview

This practical GRC/IT Audit portfolio project examines cybersecurity and operational resilience risks created by the convergence of corporate Information Technology (IT) networks with factory-floor Operational Technology (OT) environments.

The case study focuses on smart manufacturing environments where Human-Machine Interfaces (HMIs), Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs), industrial endpoints, network architecture, and production continuity must be protected against unauthorized access, legacy-system exposure, and ransomware-related disruption.

## GRC Lifecycle

Risk Identification → Risk Assessment → Framework Mapping → Control Design → Audit Testing → Evidence → Metrics → Remediation

## Project Objectives

- Identify cyber-physical risks affecting industrial manufacturing operations.
- Assess inherent and residual risk using a 1–5 likelihood and impact scale.
- Design controls for network segmentation, identity governance, and emergency isolation.
- Map controls to NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 and ISA/IEC 62443.
- Define audit procedures, evidence requirements, and review frequencies.
- Connect operational technology security with business continuity and national critical-manufacturing resilience.

## Key Risks Assessed

| Risk ID | Risk | Inherent Risk | Residual Risk |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| RSK-OT-001 | Unauthorized or unauthenticated access to factory-floor HMIs | 20/25 | 8/25 |
| RSK-OT-002 | Legacy industrial endpoints unable to support modern EDR/antivirus | 20/25 | 8/25 |
| RSK-OT-003 | Insufficient safety-aware OT incident response during ransomware | 20/25 | 6/25 |

## Core Controls

- **Unidirectional Perimeter Architecture:** Separates corporate IT and industrial OT environments using zones, conduits, DMZ architecture, and controlled diagnostic flows.
- **Industrial Asset Access Governance:** Eliminates shared and generic vendor credentials and requires unique, controlled engineer access.
- **Cyber-Physical Emergency Isolation Playbook:** Establishes safety-aware isolation and manual-override procedures when corporate identity or network services are compromised.

## Frameworks Referenced

- NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 — Guide to Operational Technology (OT) Security
- ISA/IEC 62443 — Industrial Automation and Control Systems Security
- CISA Sector Performance Goals
- Critical Manufacturing cybersecurity and resilience considerations

## Audit Testing and Evidence

The project includes audit procedures covering:

- Firewall rules and IT/OT network separation
- Jump-box access and session monitoring
- HMI and PLC authentication controls
- Factory-default credential testing
- Password vault and local account configurations
- OT incident-response tabletop exercises
- Management sign-offs, action items, and continuity-policy approvals

## National and Business Importance

Secure IT/OT convergence supports manufacturing continuity, industrial safety, supply-chain resilience, and protection of critical production environments. The project demonstrates how cybersecurity governance and audit practices can be applied to operationally sensitive manufacturing systems.
